# Business Recommendations

Fill in the numbers from `reports/segment_summary.csv` after you run the project.

| Segment | Who they are | Recommended action | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Champions | Bought recently, buy often, spend the most | Early access to new products, loyalty rewards, referral program. Do not over-discount. | Retain and turn into brand advocates |
| Loyal | Regular buyers | Upsell and cross-sell bundles, points-based loyalty tiers | Increase basket size |
| Potential Loyalists | Recent buyers with few orders | Welcome series, "second purchase" offer, product recommendations | Convert to repeat buyers |
| Need Attention | Average recency, low frequency | Time-limited offers, reminders of items viewed or bought | Prevent drift to At Risk |
| At Risk | Used to buy often, gone quiet | Personalised win-back email, discount, "we miss you" campaign | Reactivate before they are lost |
| Lost | Low recency, low frequency | Low-cost last-chance email, then stop spending on them | Cheap reactivation or list cleanup |
